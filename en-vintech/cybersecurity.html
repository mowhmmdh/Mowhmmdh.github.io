VINTECH / CYBERSECURITY

Cybersecurity focused on reducing attack surface

Operational security means access, services, traffic paths and events are controlled and observable—not simply that a security product exists on the network.

Hardening

Reduce unnecessary services, secure administrative access, apply updates and standardize critical settings.

Network Security

VLAN segmentation, firewall policy, VPN, restricted device management and controlled sensitive paths.

Visibility

Logging and monitoring practices that make faults and suspicious behavior easier to detect and investigate.

Method

Assets and traffic paths are identified first. Material risks are prioritized, then improvements are implemented with controlled operational impact. Documentation and reviewability are part of the deliverable.
