About Me

I am Mohammad Hossein Asgari Somarin, an IT specialist with practical experience in system troubleshooting, user support, network implementation, and maintaining organizational infrastructure.

I have worked with organizations such as the Ministry of Interior, Samat Newspaper, Kimia Mehr Espadana (Kimiaonline and HiAsus shopping sites), and currently at Mahex Air Cargo Services. I am passionate about networking, cybersecurity, and delivering high-quality technical solutions.

My expertise includes network management, active and passive equipment configuration, CCTV installation, hardware and software repair, and providing sustainable business continuity solutions. I am open to new challenges and collaboration with professional teams.
