Ultimate Guide to Computer Hardware Maintenance

May 2025 | Read Time: 7 min

Your computer's hardware is its beating heart. Following a few simple maintenance rules can significantly extend its lifespan and prevent costly repairs. In this article by Mohammad Hossein Asgari Somarin, a Hardware Repair Specialist, we provide a step-by-step guide to proper hardware maintenance.

1. Regular Dust Cleaning (Every 3 Months)

Dust is the #1 enemy of electronic components. Use a compressed air can or a blower to clean the case, fans, heat sinks, and power supply. Hold fans steady while cleaning to prevent bearing damage.

2. Ensure Proper Cooling

Temperatures above 80°C (176°F) are extremely harmful to your CPU and GPU. Ensure case fans are working correctly and airflow is unobstructed. Replace the CPU thermal paste every 2 years if necessary.

3. Protect Against Power Surges

Use a high-quality surge protector or UPS to safeguard components against voltage fluctuations and sudden outages. This is especially critical for hard drives and motherboards.

4. Upgrade RAM and Storage

Upgrading your RAM to 8GB or 16GB and replacing an old HDD with an SSD is one of the most effective ways to boost system speed and reduce processor stress. Also, use an external HDD for regular backups.

5. Laptop Battery Health

To extend your laptop battery's lifespan, avoid overcharging and full discharges. Keep the battery between 20% and 80% capacity. Use battery management software to monitor health.

6. Update Drivers and BIOS

Keep your hardware drivers up to date to benefit from bug fixes and performance improvements. BIOS updates can also enhance system stability, but proceed with caution.

7. Protect Ports from Dust

Use silicone dust covers for your USB, HDMI, and other ports to prevent dust ingress and potential short circuits.

8. Check Hard Drive Health with Specialized Tools

Use software like CrystalDiskInfo monthly to monitor your hard drive health. If you see errors or bad sectors, back up your data immediately.
